## Lessons from the 2026-06-27 run
- **Do NOT let subagents delegate.** [[3C - Tech & Industrials]] and [[3D - Payments & Consumer Tech]] spawned children and stalled (returned "I'll compile as they come in" with no JSON). Always include: *"Do ALL work yourself, do NOT spawn sub-agents."*
