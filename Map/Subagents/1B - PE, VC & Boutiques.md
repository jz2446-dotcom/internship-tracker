Subagent of [[Agent 1 - Elite Investing]]. Focus: PE, growth equity, VC, elite boutique IB.

**Companies:** [[TPG]], [[Bain Capital]], [[Brookfield Asset Management]], [[Ares Management]], [[General Atlantic]], [[Warburg Pincus]], [[Silver Lake]], [[Thoma Bravo]], [[Vista Equity Partners]], [[Advent International]], [[CVC Capital Partners]], [[EQT Partners]], [[Sequoia Capital]], [[Andreessen Horowitz]], [[Accel]], [[Kleiner Perkins]], [[Lightspeed Venture Partners]], [[Lazard]], [[Evercore]], [[Centerview Partners]], [[Houlihan Lokey]]

**Lesson:** Many PE/VC firms have no formal undergrad internship (mark `none`). VC `jobs.[firm].com` boards are **portfolio-company** jobs, not the fund's roles — don't confuse. Boutiques (Lazard/Evercore/Centerview/HL) have real SA programs.
